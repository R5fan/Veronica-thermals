# Veronica thermals

Thermal hotspots and a thermal-likelihood heatmap around Veronica airfield, Namibia, built from 54 self-launched glider flights (IGC files from WeGlide, Jan 2023 – Nov 2026).

**Map:** https://r5fan.github.io/Veronica-thermals/ — tap **GPS** to show your position and track (allow location in the browser).

## Files
- `files/Veronica_thermal_hotspots.cup` — hotspots as waypoints (XCSoar / OpenSoar / LX)
- `files/Veronica_thermal_hotspots.cupx` — hotspots with a heatmap picture each (SeeYou Navigator)
- `files/Veronica_thermal_heatmap_openair.txt` — heatmap areas as display-only "airspace" (GND to 1 ft AGL), OpenAir
- `files/Veronica_thermals.kmz` — heatmap and hotspots for Google Earth

## Method
A climb is circling of at least 60 s with at least 80 m gain, counted only after the engine stopped. A hotspot is a 2.5 km group of climbs used by at least 5 flights and 3 pilots. The heatmap shows, per 1 km square, how often a glider passing within 1.5 km found a climb compared with the area average.

This is a statistical pattern from a limited number of flights, not a guarantee. Use for planning only.
