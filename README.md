# Veronica thermals

Thermal hotspots and a thermal-likelihood heatmap around Veronica airfield, Namibia, built from 98 self-launched glider flights from Veronica, Kiripotib and Bitterwasser (IGC files from WeGlide, Jan 2023 – Nov 2026).

**Map:** https://r5fan.github.io/Veronica-thermals/ — tap **GPS** to show your position and track (allow location in the browser).

## Files
- `files/Veronica_thermal_hotspots.cup` — hotspots as waypoints (OpenSoar / XCSoar / LX)

## Method
A climb is circling of at least 60 s with at least 80 m gain, counted only after the engine stopped. A hotspot is a 2.5 km group of climbs used by at least 7 flights, 3 pilots and 3 different days. The heatmap shows, per 1 km square, how often a glider passing within 1.5 km found a climb compared with the area average, for the whole day or a time window (before 12:30, 12:30–15:00, after 15:00).

**Ground source** view: wind is measured from the drift of each glider's circles, and each thermal is traced back down to the terrain (SRTM-based XCSoar terrain) using climb rate plus glider sink. Sources typically sit 1–3 km upwind of where the climb was found; accuracy about 1 km.

This is a statistical pattern from a limited number of flights, not a guarantee. Use for planning only.
